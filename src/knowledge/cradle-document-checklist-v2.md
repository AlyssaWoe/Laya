# Cradle Fund Document Checklist

## 1. How to use this checklist

Use this checklist during intake. Mark each item `PRESENT`, `MISSING`, `EXPIRED`, or `UNVERIFIED`. A document that exists but cannot be authenticated is `UNVERIFIED`, not `PRESENT`.

## 2. Core pack

| Ref | Document | Required when |
|---|---|---|
| DOC-01 | Signed application form | Always |
| DOC-02 | Business plan | Always |
| DOC-03 | 24-month financial projection | Always |
| DOC-04 | Certificate of incorporation | Always |
| DOC-05 | Company constitution | Always |
| DOC-06 | Shareholder register | Always |
| DOC-07 | Audited financial statements | Grant above RM 250,000 |
| DOC-08 | Tax clearance / exemption | Always |
| DOC-09 | Pitch deck or technical annex | Always |

## 3. Validity

- Audited statements must cover the most recently completed financial year and be signed by an approved auditor.
- Tax clearance must be current for the assessment year or accompanied by a valid exemption.
- The shareholder register must be dated within 90 days of submission.

## 4. Handling gaps

Do not reject an application simply because a document is absent. Create a **missing-evidence request** listing the exact document reference, why it is required, and the deadline. The case remains `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` until the item is supplied or the reviewer records a justified exception.

## 5. Known ambiguity

This checklist and the Eligibility Standard are maintained by different programme workstreams. Where their thresholds differ, the Policy Engine must flag a conflict, apply the more conservative threshold, and route the case to a reviewer. The conflict must not be resolved silently by a model.

## 6. Reviewer notes

Record reviewer identity, date, documents inspected, exception rationale, and any follow-up commitment. Reviewer notes are part of the audit record.
